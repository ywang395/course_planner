# Prerequisites for the 26 imported SJSU courses

Researched September 14, 2026, for the courses already imported from the 2026-2027 CS BS roadmap.

The [requested catalog page](https://catalog.sjsu.edu/preview_program.php?catoid=23&poid=19252&returnto=8647&print#year1) returned a JavaScript/robot challenge. Direct retrieval also returned an AWS WAF challenge. These findings therefore are **not a verified extraction of that catalog edition**.

Nineteen courses have SJSU syllabus or departmental sources. Seven have provisional secondary listings and are explicitly marked pending SJSU catalog confirmation. Most CS sources are Fall 2026 syllabi; ENGL 1A uses Spring 2026. Departmental pages are current published guidance but may retain older terminology. The source label identifies provenance, not a guarantee that every enrollment-system restriction is covered.

## Database representation

Run after the roadmap import:

```sh
psql -d courseplanner -v ON_ERROR_STOP=1 -f src/main/resources/db/sjsu-course-prerequisite-notes.sql
```

The migration adds and populates `prerequisite_notes`, `prerequisite_source`, and `prerequisite_source_type` on the existing 26 course records. The corresponding fields are exposed by `GET /api/courses` after restarting the application. Repeating the migration updates only changed research fields and creates no duplicate courses.

To populate the `prerequisites` column with extracted course codes, run:

```sh
psql -d courseplanner -v ON_ERROR_STOP=1 -f src/main/resources/db/sjsu-course-prerequisite-codes.sql
```

The arrays contain distinct course codes mentioned as prerequisites, including alternative and conditional routes. They are a reference list, not an AND/OR expression. Concurrent calculus/discrete-math workshops and recommendations are excluded. CS 46AW is included because the CS 46B source specifically lists it as a conditional prerequisite. MATH 42 uses the precalculus entry courses from MATH 30 eligibility, not MATH 30 itself. Abbreviated alternatives such as MATH 30/30P/30PL/30X are expanded to complete codes. Referenced courses outside the original 26 are retained as codes without creating additional course records.

Twenty-one courses have course-code entries; ENGL 1A, CS 100W, PHIL 134, GEOL 1, and GEOL 7 have empty arrays. Empty does not mean unrestricted: non-course requirements and uncertainty remain in the notes. The migration updates only `prerequisites`, checks all 26 target rows, verifies the resulting arrays, and is safe to rerun.

**Eligibility limitation:** the current planner uses `containsAll`, so it incorrectly treats alternative and conditional codes as universally required. For example, CS 147 lists CS 47 and CMPE 102, but only one is needed. It also does not check grades, placement, standing, or consent. Consult `prerequisite_notes` for the full rules; these arrays alone do not provide accurate enrollment eligibility.

The seven provisional records are PHYS 50, GEOL 1, GEOL 7, BIOL 30, MATH 39, MATH 142, and MATH 161A. BIOL 30 has conflicting writing-placement categories within its secondary listing. CS 157A's syllabus has inconsistent major lists. Both discrepancies are retained in the notes.

Unnamed GE/PE/elective slots and the 24 code-only CS electives from the roadmap are outside this 26-course import.
